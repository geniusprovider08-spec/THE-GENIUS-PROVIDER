At The Genius Provider, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by The Genius Provider and how we use it, in compliance with standard data protection privacy practices and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) business platform requirements.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our website, including:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, phone number (+91 8368146862), physical address, and resume/CV details voluntarily provided when filling out our contact forms or job application forms.
  • Employment & Career Data: Job preferences, work experience, qualification details, and salary expectations submitted for recruitment purposes.
  • Log & Technical Data: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and click data.
  • Communication Data: Records and copies of your correspondence if you contact us via form, email, or WhatsApp messaging.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various legitimate business purposes, including to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and optimize our job portal and recruitment services.
  • Match candidates with suitable job vacancies and share candidate profiles with potential employer clients.
  • Send job alerts, recruitment updates, and administrative communications via Email, SMS, or WhatsApp.
  • Respond to candidate and business inquiries submitted through our contact forms.
  • Analyze website performance, user trends, and improve candidate and employer user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, and enforce our terms and conditions.

3. Information Sharing & Third-Party Services

We respect your privacy and do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties for marketing purposes. However, we may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Employer Clients: Sharing relevant resume details and contact information with registered employers for potential hiring.
  • Service Providers: Sharing data with trusted third-party service providers (e.g., hosting partners, communication services like WhatsApp API) who assist in running our website and business operations.
  • Legal Compliance: Disclosing information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. WhatsApp & Social Media Communication

By opting into our services or submitting forms, you consent to receive communication regarding job opportunities and HR solutions via WhatsApp and social media channels. You can opt out of direct communication at any time by replying "STOP" or notifying us directly.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The Genius Provider uses standard cookies and web beacons to store visitor preferences, optimize page content based on browser types, and measure marketing campaigns (including Meta Pixels/Analytics). You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We retain candidate and inquiry data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the recruitment and service goals outlined in this policy.

7. User Data Rights

Under applicable data privacy regulations, users have the following rights regarding their data:

  • The right to access and request copies of your personal data held by us.
  • The right to request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
