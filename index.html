About The Genius Provider

The Genius Provider is a recruitment and hiring company established on 22 July 2021 under the direction of Suhail Khan. Our company helps businesses solve their recruitment and hiring problems by supporting them in finding suitable candidates for their workforce requirements. Based in Preet Vihar, Delhi, we work with companies and job seekers across India.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help companies solve hiring challenges while making it easier for candidates to discover suitable employment opportunities.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a trusted recruitment network that connects businesses and job seekers across India and creates better employment opportunities.
